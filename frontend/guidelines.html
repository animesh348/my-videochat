Community Guidelines

NexTalk connects strangers around the world for video chat. To keep it safe and fun for everyone, please follow these rules.

1You must be 18 or older

NexTalk is strictly for adults. If you are under 18, please leave now. We use age verification and report any suspected underage users to authorities.

2No nudity or sexual content

Don't expose yourself, perform sexual acts, or display sexual material. This applies even if the other person consents. Violators are banned immediately.

3No harassment, hate, or threats

Don't insult people based on race, religion, gender, sexuality, nationality, disability, or appearance. Don't threaten violence. Don't bully. Be kind.

4No violence or harmful content

No real or simulated violence, self-harm, weapons, blood, gore, or anything designed to disturb. If you're in crisis, please call a local helpline.

5No illegal activity

Drugs, weapon trafficking, fraud, hacking, doxxing, sharing minors' images, and any other illegal content is forbidden.

6No spam or scams

Don't advertise, sell, beg for money, share crypto wallets, or push outside links. Don't pretend to be someone you're not for financial gain.

7Respect privacy

Don't record, screenshot, or share other people's video, voice, or personal info without their consent. Don't dox.

8No deepfakes or fake identities

Don't use AI-generated faces, prerecorded videos, or fake personas to deceive others.

9Use the report button

If someone breaks these rules, use the Report button. We review every report. Multiple reports lead to bans.

10We may ban without warning

Serious violations (nudity, threats, minors, illegal activity) result in immediate permanent bans. We may share information with law enforcement when required by law.

How we enforce these rules

  • Auto-bans: Three reports within 24 hours triggers an automatic 24-hour ban.
  • Manual review: Admins review all reports and may issue permanent bans.
  • No appeals for serious violations: Nudity, minor safety, and illegal content bans are final.
